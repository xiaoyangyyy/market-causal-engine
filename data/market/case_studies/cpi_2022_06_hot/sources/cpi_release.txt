U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Price Index release for June 2022.

The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers rose 1.3 percent in June on a seasonally adjusted basis after increasing 1.0 percent in May.

Over the last 12 months, the all items index increased 9.1 percent before seasonal adjustment — a hotter CPI print than economists expected and the largest 12-month increase in decades.

Core CPI rose 0.7 percent in June, above expectations, indicating broad inflation pressures beyond energy.

Shelter, gasoline, and food indexes contributed to the monthly increase, with energy prices remaining elevated.

The hot CPI surprise reinforced market expectations for continued Federal Reserve rate hikes and tighter financial conditions.
