Shopify Inc. (NYSE: SHOP) today announced financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2022.

Shopify reports Q2 revenue of $1.29 billion, missing Wall Street estimates, as e-commerce growth slowed sharply from pandemic-era highs.

The company lowers full-year revenue growth outlook, citing macro headwinds and softer consumer spending online.

Merchant solutions revenue growth decelerated as GMV growth slowed and merchants reduced ad spend.

Management noted inflation and pull-forward demand from 2020-2021 created a difficult comparison and weaker near-term growth trajectory.

EPS missed consensus expectations as the company continued heavy investment in fulfillment and merchant tools amid slowing top-line growth.

The company expects Q3 revenue growth to remain below prior guidance ranges.
