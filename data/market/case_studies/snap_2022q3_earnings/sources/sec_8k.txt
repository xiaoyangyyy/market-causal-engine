Snap Inc. (NYSE: SNAP) today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2022.

Snap reports Q3 revenue of $1.13 billion, missing Wall Street estimates, with digital ad revenue miss driven by advertisers cutting budgets due to macro uncertainty.

The company warned the digital ad market has worsened since prior guidance and expects Q4 revenue growth to decelerate further.

CEO noted advertisers in key verticals reduced spend faster than expected amid inflation and recession fears.

Snap said platform changes from Apple iOS privacy and macro headwinds continued to pressure ad targeting and ROI measurement.

Management expects near-term ad revenue to remain under pressure until macro conditions stabilize.
