Hindenburg Research publishes a short seller report on Nikola Corporation (NKLA) titled "Nikola: How to Parlay An Ocean of Lies Into a Partnership With the Largest Auto OEM in America."

The report alleges Nikola misled investors about its truck technology, including staging a promotional video that appeared to show a Nikola One truck driving under its own power when it was actually rolling downhill.

Hindenburg claims the company overstated progress on hydrogen fuel-cell technology and battery capabilities.

The research firm raises questions about partnerships and whether core product claims were substantiated by engineering milestones.

Nikola shares fell sharply in pre-market trading following publication as investors reassessed prior bullish narratives.

The report alleges a pattern of misleading statements to secure partnerships and inflate the stock price.

Regulatory scrutiny risk increases as short sellers highlight potential securities law concerns.
