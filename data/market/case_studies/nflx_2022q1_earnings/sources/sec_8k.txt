Netflix, Inc. (NASDAQ: NFLX) today announced financial results for its first quarter ended March 31, 2022.

The company reported a net loss of 200,000 paid memberships in Q1 versus consensus expectations for a gain of approximately 2.5 million subscribers, marking the first subscriber loss in more than a decade.

Revenue for the quarter was $7.87 billion, in line with estimates, but EPS of $3.20 missed Wall Street expectations near $3.68.

Netflix expects Q2 net paid membership additions of negative 2.0 million, below analyst expectations, reflecting slower acquisition and increased churn from password sharing and competition.

Management said paid sharing rollout and ad-supported tier plans would take time to offset near-term member growth headwinds.

The company lowers revenue outlook for Q2 on slower member growth and unfavorable foreign exchange.

CEO noted competition from streaming rivals and macro pressures weighed on engagement in certain regions.
