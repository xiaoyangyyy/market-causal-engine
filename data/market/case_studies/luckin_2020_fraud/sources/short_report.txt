Muddy Waters Research publishes a short seller report on Luckin Coffee alleging the company fabricated a material portion of reported sales.

The report claims Luckin inflated same-store sales metrics and revenue recognition through bogus transactions and corporate structure manipulation.

Muddy Waters alleges Luckin misled investors about unit economics and growth quality to support its premium valuation versus peers.

The research firm raises accounting fraud concerns and questions whether reported cash flows match operational reality.

Luckin shares plunge in pre-market trading as investors reassess prior growth narratives and audit credibility.

The report alleges a pattern of misleading statements designed to inflate the stock price and attract capital.

SEC and regulatory inquiry risk increases as short sellers highlight potential securities law violations.

Company management issued a statement disputing the allegations but offered limited detail on transaction-level evidence.
