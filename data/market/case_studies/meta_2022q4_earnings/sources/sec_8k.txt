Meta Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ: META) today reported fourth quarter and full year 2021 results.

The company reported fourth-quarter revenue of $33.67 billion, missing Wall Street estimates of $33.44 billion in a narrow miss on revenue but issuing disappointing forward guidance.

Meta expects first-quarter 2022 revenue in the range of $27 billion to $29 billion, below analyst expectations, reflecting headwinds from privacy changes including Apple's iOS tracking limits and increased competition from TikTok.

Daily active users across the Facebook family declined sequentially for the first time, with DAUs of 1.93 billion versus 1.95 billion in the prior quarter.

The company said it would significantly increase Reality Labs investment while core advertising growth slows.

EPS for the quarter was $3.67 versus consensus expectations near $3.84, an earnings miss relative to buy-side models.

Management noted advertising impressions increased but average price per ad declined due to lower monetization in certain regions.

The company lowers full-year 2022 expense growth outlook but warns revenue growth may slow materially in the near term.
