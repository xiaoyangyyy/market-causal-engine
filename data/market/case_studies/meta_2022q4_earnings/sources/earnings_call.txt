CEO Mark Zuckerberg said the company is prioritizing Reels and discovery products while navigating a meaningful transition in ads targeting efficiency.

CFO David Wehner noted that iOS changes and macro inflation in ad pricing created a stronger headwind than anticipated entering the quarter.

Management stated that advertisers in verticals such as e-commerce and consumer goods reduced budgets faster than expected.

The company expects Q1 revenue to decline or grow modestly year-over-year, a significant guidance cut relative to prior growth rates.

Executives said engagement remains strong on Instagram but monetization per user faces near-term pressure.

Management noted competitive pressure from short-form video products contributed to lower-than-expected ad revenue growth.

The outlook for 2022 reflects increased investment in the metaverse segment while core Family of Apps margins compress.
