On December 8, 2016, Palo Alto Networks, Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2016 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). At the Annual Meeting, proxies representing 91,209,982 shares of the Company’s common stock, or approximately 90% of the total shares entitled to vote, were present. The matters before the Annual Meeting were described in more detail in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the United States Securities and Exchange Commission on October 24, 2016. Proposal One – Election of Directors . The following nominees were elected as Class II directors to serve until the 2019 Annual Meeting of Stockholders or until their respective successors are duly elected and qualified.
