As a result of these changes, Accenture will change its reporting segments from five reportable operating segments: Communications, Media & Technology; Financial Services; Health & Public Service; Products; and Resources (the “Operating Groups”), to three reportable operating segments organized by Accenture’s three geographic markets: North America; Europe; and Growth Markets.

Accenture will also continue to disclose revenues by Operating Group and type of work (consulting and outsourcing) and will continue to provide the same supplemental information regarding the “New” (digital, cloud, and security services) that the company currently provides.
