EXHIBIT 99.1 Document News Release The Procter & Gamble Company One P&G Plaza Cincinnati, OH 45202 P&G ANNOUNCES FISCAL YEAR 2022 FIRST QUARTER RESULTS Net Sales +5%; Organic Sales +4%; Diluted EPS and Core EPS $1.61, each -1% MAINTAINS FISCAL YEAR 2022 GUIDANCE CINCINNATI, October 19, 2021 - The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE:PG) reported first quarter fiscal year 2022 net sales of $20.3 billion, an increase of five percent versus the prior year. Excluding the impacts of foreign exchange, acquisitions and divestitures, organic sales increased four percent. Diluted net earnings per share were $1.61, a decrease of one percent versus prior year EPS. Operating cash flow was $4.6 billion for the quarter. Adjusted free cash flow productivity was 92%.
