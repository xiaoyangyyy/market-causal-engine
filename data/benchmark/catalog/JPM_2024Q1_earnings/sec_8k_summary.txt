Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition On January 12, 2024, JPMorgan Chase & Co. (“JPMorgan Chase” or the “Firm”) reported 2023 fourth quarter net income of $9.3 billion, or $3.04 per share, compared with net income of $11.0 billion, or $3.57 per share, in the fourth quarter of 2022.
