Wichmann, chief executive officer of UnitedHealth Group. Based on strong third quarter 2019 results and its positive view for the rest of the year, the Company increased its full year net earnings outlook to $14.15 to $14.25 per share and adjusted net earnings to a range of $14.90 to $15.00 per share, both increasing $0.15 per share at the mid-point. Seniors served through Medicare Advantage grew by 315,000 over the last year to 5.23 million.
