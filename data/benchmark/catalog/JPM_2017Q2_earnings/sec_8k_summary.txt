Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition On April 13, 2017, JPMorgan Chase & Co. (“JPMorgan Chase” or the “Firm”) reported 2017 first quarter net income of $6.4 billion, or $1.65 per share, compared with net income of $5.5 billion, or $1.35 per share, in the first quarter of 2016.
