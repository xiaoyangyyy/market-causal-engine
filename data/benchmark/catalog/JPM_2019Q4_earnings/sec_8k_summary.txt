Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition On October 15, 2019 , JPMorgan Chase & Co. (“JPMorgan Chase” or the “Firm”) reported 2019 third quarter net income of $9.1 billion , or $2.68 per share, compared with net income of $8.4 billion , or $2.34 per share, in the third quarter of 2018.
