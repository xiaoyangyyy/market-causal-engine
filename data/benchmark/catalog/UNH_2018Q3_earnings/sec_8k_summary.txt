As we look ahead, we will drive our growth on the strength of practical innovations that anticipate and respond to increasing consumer expectations and clear social needs,” said David S. Wichmann, chief executive officer of UnitedHealth Group. Based on first half 2018 results and the business outlook for the balance of the year, the Company has raised its outlook for 2018 net earnings to a range of $11.80 to $12.05 per share and adjusted net earnings to a range of $12.50 to $12.75 per share, and now expects cash flows from operations to approach $15.5 billion.
