Item 2.02. Results of Operations and Financial Condition. On January 13, 2020, at the 38th Annual J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference in San Francisco, California (the “ 2020 J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference ”), Leonard S. Schleifer, M.D., Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (“ Regeneron ” or the “ Company ”), and George D. Yancopoulos, M.D., Ph.D., President and Chief Scientific Officer of Regeneron, are providing a corporate update. The presentation includes information regarding the Company’s preliminary (unaudited) U.S. net product sales of EYLEA ® (aflibercept) Injection of approximately $4.64 billion for the full year 2019 (based on preliminary (unaudited) fourth quarter 2019 U.S. net product sales of EYLEA of approximately $1.22 billion).
