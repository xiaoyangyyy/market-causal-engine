Exhibit 99.1 Tesla First Quarter 2025 Production, Deliveries & Deployments In the first quarter, we produced over 362,000 vehicles, delivered over 336,000 vehicles and deployed 10.4 GWh of energy storage products. While the changeover of Model Y lines across all four of our factories led to the loss of several weeks of production in Q1, the ramp of the New Model Y continues to go well. Thank you to all our customers, employees, suppliers, shareholders and supporters who helped us achieve these results.
