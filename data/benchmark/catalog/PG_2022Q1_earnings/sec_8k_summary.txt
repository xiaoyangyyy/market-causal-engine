Excluding the impacts of foreign exchange, acquisitions and divestitures, organic sales also increased six percent. Diluted net earnings per share were $1.66, an increase of 13% versus prior year GAAP EPS and an increase of one percent versus prior year Core EPS. Operating cash flow was $5.1 billion for the quarter. Adjusted free cash flow productivity was 106%.
